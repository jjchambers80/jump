# Getting Started — Create Your Organization

## Overview

Everything in Jump belongs to an **organization**: your events, venues, tickets, orders, customers, participants, online store and settings are all private to it. Creating one takes about a minute and does not require a card.

## Create your organization

1. **Sign in** at `/auth/signin` with a magic link or Google. New accounts have no organization yet, so opening the admin area takes you straight to signup.
2. Already running an organization? Open the organization menu in the top-right of the admin area and choose **Create organization**. Signup opens in a new tab; your current tab keeps working.
3. **Name your organization.** This is what attendees see on your store, tickets and emails. The store handle (the URL slug) is generated from the name; you can change both later under **Online store**.
4. **Tell us about your events** (optional). A few quick screens — what you want to do, what kind of events you run, how many a year, typical attendance, and where you are moving from. Pick as many or as few as you like, or press **Skip** in the top-right to skip the whole thing. Answers only tailor your setup guide.
5. **Done.** You land on the dashboard of your new organization. If you started from the organization menu, the tab you came from now lists the new organization too.

## The setup guide

A new organization's dashboard shows a set of cards until you have finished the basics:

| Card | What it does | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **Create your first event** | Opens the event form | Your first event exists (draft is fine) |
| **Choose your store design** | Theme mode, brand colour, logo | Any of them is set |
| **You're ready to accept payments** / **Connect your Stripe account** | Payment methods and statement name, or connecting your own Stripe account | Payments reviewed / Stripe connected |
| **Add your business details** | Legal name and address for receipts and tax reports | Both are filled in |
| **Claim your web address** | Your own domain for the store | A domain is active |
| **Open vendor & sponsor applications** | Shown when you said you run applications | A form exists |

Press **Dismiss guide** to hide it at any time; it does not come back.

## Unfinished signups

If you close the tab midway, opening signup again resumes where you left off. An unfinished organization is not shown in the organization menu; you can hold up to three at once. Jump staff can discard abandoned ones.
