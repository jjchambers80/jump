# How to Purchase Tickets

A step-by-step guide for purchasing event tickets on Jump Tickets.

## Overview

Jump Tickets lets you browse upcoming events, purchase tickets securely through Stripe, and receive QR codes for event entry. Your tickets are always accessible from your account.

## Step 1: Browse Events

1. Visit the Jump Tickets homepage at your event organizer's URL
2. You'll see a list of upcoming published events
3. Each event card shows:
   - Event name and date/time
   - Venue location
   - Ticket price
   - Number of tickets remaining
4. Click on an event to view full details

## Step 2: Select Tickets

1. On the event detail page, review:
   - Full event description
   - Date, time, and venue
   - Ticket price
   - Available capacity
2. Use the quantity selector to choose how many tickets (1–10 per purchase)
3. Enter your email address
4. Click **"Buy Tickets"**

> **Note**: If the event shows "Sold Out", tickets are no longer available.

## Step 3: Complete Payment

1. You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout page
2. Enter your payment details:
   - Card number
   - Expiration date
   - CVC
   - Billing information
3. Click **"Pay"** to complete the purchase

> **Security**: All payments are processed through Stripe. Jump Tickets never stores your card details.

## Step 4: Receive Your Tickets

After successful payment, you'll be redirected to a confirmation page showing:

- Your ticket(s) with unique QR codes
- Event details (name, date, venue)
- Purchase confirmation

An email with your tickets and QR codes will also be sent to your email address.

## Step 5: Access Your Tickets Later

### Creating an Account

1. Click **"Sign In"** in the navigation bar
2. Click **"Create account"** on the login page
3. Enter your email, name, and a password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Click **"Register"**

### Viewing Purchase History

1. Log in to your account
2. Click **"My Tickets"** in the navigation bar
3. Your tickets are grouped by event, showing:
   - Event name, date, and venue
   - Number of tickets per event
   - Ticket status (Valid, Redeemed, or Expired)
4. Click on any ticket to view its full details and QR code

### Downloading QR Codes

1. Navigate to **My Tickets** → click a specific ticket
2. On the ticket detail page, click **"Download QR Code"**
3. The QR code will be saved as a PNG image to your device

## Ticket Statuses

| Status       | Meaning                                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Valid**    | Ticket is active and can be used for event entry |
| **Redeemed** | Ticket has been scanned and used at the event    |
| **Expired**  | The event has passed; ticket is no longer valid  |

## Troubleshooting

### Payment Failed

- Verify your card details are correct
- Ensure sufficient funds are available
- Try a different payment method
- Contact your bank if the issue persists

### Didn't Receive Email

- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify the email address you used during purchase
- Log in to your account and access tickets from **"My Tickets"**

### Event Sold Out During Checkout

- If tickets sell out while you're completing payment, your card will not be charged
- You'll see an error message and can return to browse other events

## Need Help?

Contact the event organizer directly for event-specific questions. For technical issues with the platform, reach out to Jump Tickets support.
